# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Cerbyl currently serves individual learners through a B2C learning workspace. The institutional extension adds students enrolled in classes and teachers, professors, and teaching staff who manage those classes. Each account now has a server-authoritative learner, student, or educator role and is routed to its corresponding workspace after authentication.

## Product Purpose

Cerbyl is a unified learning workspace that connects an AI tutor, notes, course materials, flashcards, quizzes, learning paths, mastery analysis, and spaced review. The institutional experience connects student learning activity with teacher-authored course structure, assignments, feedback, and interventions.

## Positioning

Cerbyl's differentiating mechanism is a continuous learning loop in which course context, tutor interactions, practice results, mastery evidence, teacher interventions, and spaced review inform one another instead of living in separate tools.

## Operating Context

Individual learners use the existing Cerbyl Learn dashboard. Students use class-oriented dashboards to see courses, assignments, approved materials, practice, feedback, and personal progress. Teachers and professors use an educator dashboard to manage classes, create learning activities, review submissions, identify misconceptions, and intervene with students. Test accounts and a local demo institution allow both ends to be validated before invitation and provisioning flows are selected.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing product includes authentication, an AI tutor, document-grounded context, notes, audio/video and slide processing, flashcards with spaced repetition, question generation, quizzes, weakness detection, knowledge maps, learning paths, analytics, gamification, social activities, and web and mobile clients. Existing B2C authentication and the Cerbyl Learn dashboard continue to work. Student and Educator workspaces reuse the incumbent theme system and draw from persistent organization, membership, term, course, section, enrollment, material, assignment, submission, and announcement records.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Cerbyl. The existing visual implementation is the design authority: dark graphite and near-black surfaces, warm gold accent, Inter typography, geometric grid motifs, subtle grain, precise data-dense layouts, and restrained use of glow and depth.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the working web application, mobile application, backend APIs, analytics, learning models, course-adjacent learning features, automated tests, institutional database models and migration, role-aware APIs, and an idempotent local demo seed.

## Product Principles

- Preserve one connected learning loop across learner, student, and educator experiences.
- Prioritize the next useful action over exposing the entire feature catalogue.
- Give students clarity, momentum, and understandable progress.
- Give educators actionable teaching signals instead of generic engagement metrics.
- Keep AI assistance visible, governed, and subordinate to teacher judgment.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

New operational surfaces should support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced motion, responsive layouts, semantic landmarks, readable contrast, and clear non-color status labels.
